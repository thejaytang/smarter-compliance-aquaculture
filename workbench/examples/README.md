# Workbench demonstration

`example.html` is the original reading material. `example.json` describes its exact source text, decomposition and interpretation content.

The three cases cover anchoring-line conditions and alternative demands; fictional fish removal with nested exceptions and 2–3 of 4 actions; and the translated sea-lice illustration with parallel temperature/counting Requirements. Fourth-pane examples are supplied for the four primary Requirements. Proposed evidence mappings and unresolved questions remain distinct from source wording.

On the authoring installation, open **Material review → example (PE001)**, then select a primary Requirement to view its interpretation in the fourth pane. It is pinned first for the requesting reviewer. This is a saved personal demonstration, not an adopted or reviewed legal determination.

These files alone do not create database records on another installation. The [installation checkpoint](../../project-support/workbench-example-20260916/RESULTS.md) records the local material and verification. No official legal authenticity, current-law status or actual compliance result is asserted.
