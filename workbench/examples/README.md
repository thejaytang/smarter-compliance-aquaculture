# Current Workbench example: ADF-10

The current example is entirely English, based on the English ADF-10 JSON supplied by the user. `example.html` and `example.md` present the corresponding English demonstration excerpt; `example.json` remains the supplied JSON unchanged. `example-workbench.json` records the four-column normalization and check design. This is a demonstration translation, not an official English legal publication. The previous Norwegian original and its saved work remain in source/material history.

Open **Material review → example (PE001)** on the authoring installation. It is pinned first for the requesting reviewer. The former demonstration remains available as an older source/material version.

## Four entries

- **R1:** the shared operational-journal obligation. Its Subrequirement links require all three entries R2, R3 and R4.
- **R2:** incoming/outgoing animals and products, including both origin and destination. The location group retains `[2,2]`.
- **R3:** mortality per production unit, qualified by relevance to the production method.
- **R4:** results of completed health inspections. The five listed information categories retain `[5,5]`.

Each of the four source passages is one separate Requirement. R2–R4 contain only their own bullet; the shared lead-in is not copied into them. R1 references all three through Subrequirement links. Their Logic fields explain the shared context inherited through R1; absent literal subjects or modal verbs are not invented. Local grammatical subjects and source-stated verbs are still decomposed within each passage. Group counts apply to listed required categories; they do not cap records or forbid additional journal information.

## Verbs and local groups

R2 marks **brought into** and **removed from** as the two verbal phrases, with animals/products as their shared passive subjects and the establishment as the shared location complement. Both directions and both categories must be covered in the records; an individual record is not required to belong to both categories or both directions.

R4 keeps five result categories. **Examinations performed** and **treatments carried out** are grouped and split into their subjects and passive verbs. **Completed** qualifies health inspections; **sampling** names an information category. R3 contains a nominal mortality expression and a relevance qualifier, with no verb to invent. These descriptive phrases do not add duties to move animals or perform medical activities. The obligation to keep updated information remains in R1.

## Fourth pane

Scope, Conditions and Demands retain source wording. Their editable Logic fields explain object identification, applicability and evidence checks in English. The applicability expression is `NOT (covered_by_chapter_4 OR covered_by_chapter_5 OR covered_by_chapter_6)`, presented as a proposed logical interpretation, not an executable Site Model query. The linked chapters are not included here, and the excerpt gives no fixed update interval. Those gaps remain explicit. R1 composes its child checks with AND; missing evidence never becomes a passing result.
