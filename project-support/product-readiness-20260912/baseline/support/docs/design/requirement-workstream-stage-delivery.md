## Deliver now

Deliver System1, System2A, System2B and the shared workbench as a stable local system that completes representative real work through machine processing and necessary human review. Reuse verified migration, preservation and recovery evidence. Do not repeat a completed migration or clear all business Pending as a condition of delivery.

Priority order is data integrity and truthful states; usable missing functionality and human paths; representative end-to-end delivery/recovery; cost-effective automatic quality improvements; then difficult cases and formal quality optimization.

Required capabilities:

- Preserve source identities, original files, versions, INCLUDE decisions and named-human history. Keep incomplete, unreadable or unavailable originals explicit, with an actionable source follow-up path. A contents list, summary or error page is not a complete body.
- Use owning relational databases for business state and decisions. Generate Excel one way, with coalesced background updates, consistent versions, retry/recovery and no normal readback of edits.
- Retain machine extraction/checking and normal local operation without an API. A preserves source content and positions before B classification; photos/illustrations remain whole images, while scanned body text has an actual manual transcription/correction path.
- In the shared workbench, view original evidence and context, enter/correct missing text, repair necessary table/footnote/cross-page structure or use a workable manual alternative, save decisions and inspect history. No daily database editing, engineering scripts or Excel decisions are acceptable substitutes.
- B accepts adequately checked A, including named-human confirmation. Support advisory requirements and correction of both false positives and false negatives. Keep the complete parent, exact source associations and reversible/configurable provisional subitems/counting.
- Separate unknown machine confidence from human confirmation. Missing calibration does not block a fully checked human result with no unresolved substantive issue. Explicit errors, conflicting evidence and unfinished drafts remain protected.
- Preserve decisions and newer results across retry/restart/recovery. Partial, failed or draft work cannot appear complete. Relevant changes invalidate affected downstream results, preserving prior history.
- Exercise weekly System1 5, A 20 and B 5 sampling, original-side A coverage, positive/negative B populations, shortfalls, repair/follow-up and history. System3 stays off. A verified mechanism with documented activation can ship while actual scheduling awaits authorization.
- Demonstrate representative text, scan-path, complex-table, cross-page, HTML and XLSX tasks through machine/manual work and output. Clearly labelled test materials may verify an otherwise unavailable real-sample mechanism; they do not establish real-source accuracy.
- Record representative machine/manual share, operation steps and elapsed work. High manual share alone is not failure. Unclear, blocked or developer-dependent daily operations are failures. Proposed numerical budgets are not automatic release blockers.

System3 ontology work, Evidence extraction, site matching and final compliance determination remain outside this stage. Protected external operations, publication, scheduler activation, model enablement and material transmission retain their authorization boundaries.

## Defer without misrepresentation

Formal calibration, comprehensive independent precision/recall and verifier error rates, large held-out acceptance, broader real-sample coverage, difficult automatic extraction quality, lower reviewer burden, permanent peer splitting rules and optional model/multi-agent comparisons may be future improvements. Existing exposed development samples remain exposed. Do not invent confidence or claim independently proven accuracy.

An unresolved business item may remain Pending when its next action is clear and usable. The system may be delivered while individual sources remain unprocessed. Distinguish system functional delivery, one source's processing completion and independent automatic-quality acceptance.

## Stop and audit rules

Count historical attempts: normally stop after three consecutive hypothesis/verification repair rounds for the same automatic-quality issue. If a usable manual route exists and integrity/state truth are safe, record the issue in [future improvements](../plans/requirement-workstream-improvements.md) and proceed. Resume only with new useful evidence and a bounded credible benefit. Data loss, incorrect overwrite, false completion and an unusable main workflow must instead receive a simpler verified repair or be reported as genuine blockers.

Completion requires adequate evidence for every mandatory capability and representative end-to-end delivery, with no unresolved main-flow or integrity blocker. It does not require empty Pending, perfect automatic extraction or formal independent quality acceptance. The final report separates verified functions, known limits, manual interventions, outstanding authorizations/inputs and future improvements.
