Success means agreed samples contain no unresolved missed/incorrect items and the corresponding scoring method has validated applicability. Failure must be assigned to source availability, parser fidelity, coverage/classification, or routing policy before changing that layer. Do not lower a threshold or hide a pending task to pass.

Large complete PDFs, external model transmission, production-source acceptance and consumer deployment are separate operational steps. The implementation does not manufacture independent human labels, acquire missing originals, enable an unspecified service or claim System3 has run.
