# Local layout consolidation

2026-09-17. The application root is the entire `05_Working area of requirements side` directory. Outer project folders are unchanged. Product entry guides and installation are maintained once at the root. Historical development evidence lives under `validation`, design visuals under `design`, and operational caches under `workbench/runtime/cache`. Legacy source/history bytes are retained, not deleted.

Historical reports and manifests retain the paths they originally recorded; the mapping below locates their retained files. Legacy scripts describe the former layout and are not active product entry points. Current runnable tests remain under `workbench/tests`.

Additionally, 49 generated cache directories beneath backend, deployment and tests were relocated to `workbench/runtime/cache/legacy-code`, preserving their relative paths. Their individual mappings are recorded in `cache_moves` in [moves.json](../validation/architecture-20260917/directory-followup/moves.json). The unified launcher routes new Python bytecode to `workbench/runtime/cache/python`.
