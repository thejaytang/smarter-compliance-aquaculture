For aquaculture establishments that are not subject to specific record-keeping provisions in Chapters 4, 5 and 6, the operational journal shall at a minimum contain updated information on:

  • aquaculture animals and aquaculture animal products brought into and removed from the aquaculture establishment, including place of origin and place of destination,
  • mortality per production unit relevant to the production method, and
  • results of completed health inspections: number of completed health inspections, sampling, examinations performed, diagnoses and treatments carried out.
