## 6. Adaptive execution and completion decision

The [bounded plan](../plans/requirement-workstream-stage-v2.md) gives exact milestones, hypotheses, evidence and branching. It starts with runtime audit and early baseline, repairs at most **1–2 high-value root causes**, and reserves the final **3 hours of the 8-hour window** for freeze/retest/report/Canva/handoff. A 6-hour closure is allowed only when evidence is ready and at least the final 2 hours remain protected. Never spend the full budget on PDF optimization.

For each defect: observe -> distinguish symptom/root cause -> state one discriminating hypothesis -> run the cheapest useful experiment -> record before/after on identical frozen cases and unchanged controls -> retain/revert/adjust. Count all historical attempts at that same cause. After **3 consecutive ineffective attempts**, STOP that route; restart only with new evidence that changes the diagnosis, a new bounded test and remaining authorized time. A validated manual alternative may preserve useful function, but does not erase the quality or automation failure. Integrity loss, false completion and main-flow breaks must be repaired or remain hard blockers.

At checkpoints use CONTINUE, ADJUST, BACKTRACK, PIVOT or STOP, with scope/evidence/failure/open input/next action. Keep near steps detailed and later work provisional. Resume across sessions from the canonical goal, plan and state, not a reset attempt count or a new migration. If scope must shrink, retain confirmed core functionality and identify the exact unmet quality/material claims; no silent removal of difficult samples, relaxed thresholds or renamed automation success.
