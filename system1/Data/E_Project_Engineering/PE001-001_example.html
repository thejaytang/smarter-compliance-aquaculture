2. Fictional fish-removal example: nesting and quantity constraints

The human shall remove the fish if either the tank is red or all of the following are true: the water is hot, the fish is sick, and the pump is off.

Small fish are exempt from the removal requirement when the water is cold.

This exemption does not apply when the fish is sick.

When removing the fish, the human shall do at least two and no more than three of the following: use a net; wear gloves; close the door; call for help.

3. Sea-lice clause: user-provided translation from the explanatory figures

Seawater temperature at a depth of three metres shall be measured at least once every week.

The number of sea lice shall be counted at least every 7 days at temperatures equal to or above 4°C, and at least every 14 days at temperatures below 4°C. Broodstock are exempt from the counting requirement at temperatures below 4°C. Sea lice counting shall be carried out in accordance with the requirements of Annex 1.
